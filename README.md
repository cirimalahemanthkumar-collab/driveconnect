\# DriveConnect



DriveConnect is a full-stack driving school marketplace platform.



\## Modules



\- Customer website

\- Partner driving school dashboard

\- Admin dashboard

\- Authentication and role-based access

\- Driving school registration and approval

\- Course management

\- Marketplace

\- Booking flow

\- Payment tracking

\- Class scheduling

\- Reviews and ratings

\- Complaints and support

\- Notifications

\- Payout management

\- Document verification
